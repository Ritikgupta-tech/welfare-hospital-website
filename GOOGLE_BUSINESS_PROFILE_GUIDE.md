## 6. Review & Reputation Management Strategy (Ethical & Non-Manipulative)

### Google & Medical Council Rules:
- **Never purchase fake reviews** or use automated review-generation bots. Google’s spam filters actively suspend local profiles with suspicious review patterns.
- Do not offer discounts, gifts, or financial incentives in exchange for 5-star ratings.

### Ethical Review Acquisition:
1. Generate the short Google Review link:
   - Go to GBP Dashboard → **"Ask for reviews"** → Copy `https://g.page/r/.../review`.
2. Share with real discharged patients via SMS or WhatsApp with an honest message:
   > *"Thank you for visiting Welfare Hospital, Agra. If our medical guidance helped you, please take 30 seconds to share your authentic feedback on our Google profile: [Link]. Your review helps other patients in Agra find dependable care."*
3. Always respond professionally to both positive and critical feedback with patient privacy in mind (never reveal patient medical conditions in public replies).
